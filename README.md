# Iron Ledger

**Your personal fitness tracker.** Track workouts, build routines, monitor progress, and keep your training history organized.

🌐 **[Open Iron Ledger](https://raagasura1.github.io/Iron-Ledger/)**

## Getting Started

1. Open Iron Ledger using the link above.
2. Choose your training mode: **Gym**, **Calisthenics**, or **Cardio**.
3. Start a workout, select a routine, or enter a workout you have already completed.
4. Add your exercises and record your sets, repetitions, weights, or other relevant measurements.
5. Finish and save your workout to review it later.

## Managing Your Data

**Back up your data regularly.**

1. Open the app's **Settings** using the settings icon.
2. Find the **Export and backup** section.
3. Select **Full backup (JSON)** to save a backup of your app data.
4. Keep the downloaded backup file somewhere safe.

To restore a backup, open Settings and select **Restore backup**.

You can also export workout history and body measurements as CSV files.

**Important:** Your workout data is stored in your browser's local storage. It is not automatically synchronized across devices. Clearing browser data or using a different browser or device may make your existing data unavailable. Export a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data.
